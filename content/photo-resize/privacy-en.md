Photo Resize processes photos on your device. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network permission. The developer does not collect your photos or other personal data.

Only the photos you choose or share with the app are accessed. Original files are never modified. Resized copies omit location and other source photo metadata by default. Preserve metadata keeps supported EXIF details such as available location, capture dates, camera information and copyright in resized JPEG and PNG copies. It is off for each new selection. Orientation and dimensions are updated for the resized pixels. App-specific metadata (including XMP and maker notes), embedded thumbnails and other unsupported details are not copied. Sharing Original preserves the original file and all its metadata. In a multi-photo selection, already-small and animated images are shared unchanged, including metadata.

Temporary copies stay in the app’s private cache. On subsequent launches, copies unused for 24 hours are removed. Android may clear the cache earlier. Copies are excluded from app backup.

When you tap a size and choose another app in Android’s sharing menu, that app receives temporary access to the selected files. Its handling of the photo is governed by its own privacy practices.

A system photo provider may download a cloud photo before giving it to Photo Resize. Photo Resize itself does not upload or download photos.
