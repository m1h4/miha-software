## Find your station

Browse by region or programming, or search by station name or city. Listen to HRT’s national and regional programs, Radio Tvornica, bravo!, TOP RADIO, Radio Dalmacija and more. Star your favorites and put them in your own order; recently played stations are always close by.

## Keep listening

Radio keeps playing while you use other apps or lock your screen. Control it from the Lock Screen or Control Center, send it to a speaker with AirPlay, or set a sleep timer for 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. If your connection briefly drops, Radio tries to reconnect automatically.

## Remember a good song

See what is playing and browse a station’s recent songs when the broadcaster provides them. Save a song to find it later, search for it in Apple Music, or share it. Your favorite stations and saved songs sync between your devices through your own iCloud account.

## Free, without tracking

There is no Radio account to create, no tracking and no analytics. Radio adds no ads of its own; broadcasts may include the stations’ commercials. Streams come directly from the broadcasters, who also supply any song information. Radio is an independent app and is not affiliated with the stations.
