## Choose what fits

Set up a widget for work and another for home. Each can show a different set of calendars and look ahead 7, 30 or 70 days. Choose Compact or Comfortable spacing. Turn on **Scroll through events** to browse beyond the last visible row.

## Open the agenda

The companion app groups events by day and uses each calendar’s color. Browse the agenda, manage widgets and change settings in English or Croatian, on phones, tablets and foldables.

## Privacy

Events is free for Android 10 and later. It reads calendars already on your device and has no accounts, ads or analytics.
